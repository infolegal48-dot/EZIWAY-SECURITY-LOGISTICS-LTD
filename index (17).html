    What we store

    Three asset classes. One standard of care.

    We accept only gold, precious metals and precious stones. No documents, no general valuables, no mixed cargo. A narrow scope lets us build the vault, the handling rules and the insurance around the assets we hold.

    Gold

    Bars, coins, doré and refined bullion held in individually numbered, sealed containers.

    • Weighed & recorded on intake
    • Serial numbers logged
    • Segregated or bulk

    Precious metals

    Silver, platinum, palladium and other investment-grade metals in bar, ingot or granule form.

    • Assay documents on file
    • Moisture-controlled bays
    • Lot-level tracking

    Gemstones

    Diamonds, rubies, emeralds, sapphires and other cut or rough stones, packed and sealed by lot.

    • Grading reports attached
    • Individual lot envelopes
    • Viewing room on request

    Secure logistics

    Armoured, tracked transport between your location and the vault, with a two-person crew and sealed handover.

    • Live route tracking
    • Signed handover at both ends
    • Insured in transit
    Security

    Four layers between the street and your asset.

    Each layer works on its own. An intruder, or an error, has to get past all four, and every crossing is recorded.

    1. L1

      Controlled perimeter

      Fenced grounds, vehicle barriers, perimeter cameras and a single screened entrance for people and armoured vehicles.

    2. L2

      Hardened building

      Reinforced structure, mantrap entry, 24-hour on-site guards and an alarm centre that never closes.

    3. L3

      Vault chamber

      Rated vault door with time-lock, seismic and thermal sensors, dual-control access and no lone entry.

    4. L4

      Individual container

      Your assets sit in a sealed, numbered container. Opening it takes your authorisation and two of our officers.

    Chain of custody

    From your hands to the vault, and back again.

    Every handover is documented and counter-signed. You get a receipt at each stage, so the record of who held what, and when, is never in question.

    Verify & onboard

    Identity and source-of-assets checks, then a custody agreement stating what you are depositing and its declared value.

    Custody agreement

    Collect or receive

    Your items arrive by armoured transport or you deliver them in person. We count, weigh and photograph in front of you.

    Intake receipt

    Seal & vault

    Items are sealed in numbered containers and placed in your storage unit by two officers. The seal number is recorded.

    Seal register entry

    Monitor & report

    Continuous monitoring, periodic inspections and a statement of holdings sent on a schedule you choose.

    Holdings statement

    Release

    Release needs your written instruction and identity check. We confirm seals are intact before anything leaves.

    Release certificate
